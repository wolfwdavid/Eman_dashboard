## Organization and mission
Diversity Includes Disability (DID) advances intersectional disability equity, for and by disabled
people. Through training, consulting, representation, and public speaking, DID helps organizations
move beyond compliance to genuine inclusion, centering the leadership of disabled people of color.
Founded and led by Eman Rimawi-Doster in New York City. Legal status: 501(c)(3) pending (do not
promise tax-deductibility). Website: https://www.diversityincludesdisability.org/ (moved from Wix to
GitHub Pages on 2026-09-24; the old Wix site is archived, not deleted).

## Founder: Eman Rimawi-Doster
Eman Rimawi-Doster is a disability-justice advocate, organizer, speaker, educator, and writer, and the
founder and Executive Director of Diversity Includes Disability. She is a Black and Palestinian woman,
born and raised in NYC, a bilateral (double) amputee since 2014, and lives with lupus. For 26 years she
has organized around diversity, equity, and inclusion in disenfranchised communities through art,
writing, fashion, creativity, and organizing. She taught creative writing, community organizing, and
political science workshops to youth in the city. She led the Access-A-Ride campaign at New York
Lawyers for the Public Interest and is one of New York's strongest voices on accessible transit,
voting rights, and health equity. She also serves as Executive Director of the Harlem Independent
Living Center. Since 2014 she has launched an adaptive clothing line and works as a poet and
graphic-novel creator. She has spoken at City & State, Non-Profit Con, the University of Michigan,
Riders Alliance, DC Comics, Skybound, and Fordham University, and delivered a Disability Inclusion
training for Manhattan Borough President Mark Levine.

## Services and what we do
Four core services, delivered for and by disabled people:
1. Intersectional Disability Equity and Inclusion trainings and facilitation. Sliding-scale,
   individualized, interactive workshops for businesses, organizations, schools, hospitals,
   corporations, and any place that includes or wants to include disabled people. Moves teams beyond
   compliance toward genuine inclusion.
2. Disability Consulting. Disabled-led guidance on access, policy, and inclusive practice.
3. Modeling for Representation. Eman represents multiple marginalized communities; accurate
   representation in ads grows a business and encourages disabled customers.
4. Speaker and Panelist. Keynotes, panels, conferences, and public speaking on disability justice,
   accessible transit, voting rights, and health equity.
Current site page: https://www.diversityincludesdisability.org/programs/

## Art, writing, and creative work
Eman has made art since her father gave her a Fuji film camera in 1995, when she was 11: hundreds of
thousands of photographs, thousands of poems, stories and articles, and hundreds of drawings,
paintings and mixed-media pieces. The old site's Mixed Media Portfolio listed works such as
"A Black and Palestinian Flower Grows in Harlem", "Phoenix Rising", "One Leg, Two Legs",
"Deep in Curly Thoughts", "Holy, Muqadas, Holy", "Qalbi" and "Warada". Gallery projects included
"Action for Access: Envisioning an Accessible NYC" and the "Odera Editorial / Vertige Adaptive" shoot
(17 editorial photos, archived). She welcomes submissions, collaborations, and creative projects.
